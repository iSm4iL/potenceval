## People and currency checks

- [Nobel Foundation: Mother Teresa](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/peace/1979/teresa/biographical/) explicitly places her birth in present-day Skopje; MK association is birthplace, not citizenship or ethnic identity.
- [IOC: Montréal 1976](https://www.olympics.com/ioc/news/montreal-celebrates-30th-anniversary-of-1976-olympic-games) confirms Comăneci's 10.0. Recognition judgments and the other initial replacement biographies were not all independently checked in this pass.
- [National Bank: monetary units](https://www.nbt.tj/en/banknotes_coins/) confirms 100 dirams per somoni. The displayed clue intentionally suppresses the identifying main unit.
- [ECB: euro introduction](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2026/html/ecb.pr260101~c830245e42.en.html) confirms BG uses euro from 1 January 2026.
- BO's centavo description follows the existing dossier unit; MONEY is a text label, never an article or banknote image. EC may say “Доллар США” because this does not reveal EC. Retained generic unit names such as гривна, злотый, тугрик are legitimate clues; names derived directly from the target country are suppressed.
- Birthplace qualifiers are explicit for Chagall, Mother Teresa, Baryshnikov, Timur, Buddha and Mercury. Buddha's association is qualified as traditional. No citizenship is inferred for medieval people.
- Omission decisions deliberately exclude ancestry-only WS and a foreign co-prince for AD. Absence of an alternative is not a claim that the country has no accomplished people. Original people not overridden or omitted remain outside this module's audit.
