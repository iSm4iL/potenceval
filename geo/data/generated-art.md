# Original game illustrations

Created 7 November 2026.

- `assets/detective-desk.webp` is an AI-generated walnut desk with lamp and stationery, used behind the menu.
- `assets/food-art/dograma.webp`, `sancocho.webp` and `palusami.webp` are AI-generated gouache illustrations. They are labelled as illustrations in the clue and image viewer.
- Recipe references are [One Turkmen Kitchen](https://turkmenkitchen.com/en/recipe/dograma), [INCAP Panama](https://bvssan.incap.int/panama/) and [Samoa Tourism](https://www.samoa.travel/experience/foodies/foods-to-try/).
- `banknotes.js` draws original fictional game tokens keyed only by the displayed currency clue. These are labelled illustrations and do not imitate real banknotes.
